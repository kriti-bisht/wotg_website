Welcome to Wisdom on the GO!

Our Big Hairy Audacious Goal: To get rid of human-skills training that is generic, theoretical, and takes forever to become useful… Instead, we focus on a real work situation that matters to you.

What Happens Here?

You bring a real work situation, I ask questions, we unpack what is really going on. Then I identify:

  • The Hero Skill — many human-skills may be relevant, but this is the one most likely to create the biggest shift in your situation right now.
  • The things quietly getting in the way of your Hero Skill showing up the way it should.

Finally, we’ll do a short, personalized training session — a Learning Atom — to enable your success.

In Scope

Real work situations: current challenges, recent experiences, upcoming conversations, stakeholder headaches, leadership dilemmas, team issues, and… everything in between.

Out of Scope

  • Personal life situations.
  • Generic training requests.
  • Theory without a real situation.

Some Tiny Asks

  • Be generous with details — the more I understand your situation, the better I can help.
  • Give the process a chance — I may ask unexpected questions or arrive at conclusions you were not expecting. Hear me out before deciding whether I’ve got it right.
  • Share your feedback. If something was brilliant, tell us. If something was rubbish, definitely tell us. Both are immensely valuable ❤️

Privacy

Your conversation with WOTG is private and will not be shared with your manager, employer, HR team, or other participants.

So what does WOTG actually do?

Not just training. The complete loop.

Wisdom on the GO! is an end-to-end human capability development architecture that diagnoses the human skills people need, develops them in context, and tracks what happens when they apply them.

STEP 01DiagnoseFind the skill that matters — and what is stopping it from showing up.WOTG identifies the human skill most relevant to a given workplace situation and diagnoses the underlying friction affecting its demonstration.
STEP 02DevelopGive each person only the learning they need, when they need it.WOTG delivers rapid, hyper-personalized, situation-specific learning built around the user’s role, context and real work.
STEP 03MeasureTrack whether learning actually changes what happens at work.WOTG follows application, captures what happened when the learning was used, and builds evidence of capability movement over time.
STEP 04ReportSee whether your human-skills investment is moving the business.WOTG connects skill development and confirmed application with the KPIs those skills were intended to influence — so you can see where to invest more, change course, or stop spending.
